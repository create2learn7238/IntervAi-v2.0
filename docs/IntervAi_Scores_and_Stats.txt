IntervAi - Scores and Statistics Breakdown

1. AI Score (Overall Score)
- How it's counted: When a candidate answers a question, their response is sent to Google Gemini AI alongside an "Ideal Answer Key." The AI evaluates the answer for technical depth, correctness, and understanding, and returns a rating from 0 to 10. 
- Fallback: If the AI service fails or is offline, a local "Keyword Engine" calculates a score based on the percentage of technical keywords matched between the candidate's answer and the ideal answer.
- Final Average: A candidate's overall AI Score (e.g., 7.5/10) is simply the average of these individual answer ratings across all of their completed interviews.

2. Sub-scores (Technical, Communication, Problem Solving)
- How they're counted: In the current version of the platform, the backend explicitly equalizes these sub-scores. Your Technical, Communication, and Problem Solving scores are set to perfectly match your overall average AI Score. (The code notes this was done to remove arbitrary/fake subscores that lacked dedicated AI rubrics).

3. Detailed Answer Metrics (Confidence, Clarity, Pace, Depth, Vocabulary)
- How they're counted: During the AI grading phase for a specific question, the AI evaluates the transcript text and assigns proportional numeric scores (from 0 to 100) for each metric based on the substance, vocabulary used, and structure of that specific answer.

4. Trust Score (Anti-Cheating Integrity)
- How it's counted: Every interview starts with a perfect score of 100. Points are strictly deducted based on logged proctoring violations:
    - Camera Off: -15 points
    - Mic Off: -10 points
    - Browser Resize: -10 points
    - Exiting Fullscreen: -5 points
    - Switching Tabs: -5 points
    - Window Hidden: -5 points
    - Video Analysis Deductions: Looking away (-15), no face detected (-25), or multiple faces detected (-20).
- Status Levels: Excellent (90+), Good (75-89), Warning (50-74), Critical (<50).

5. Readiness Percentage
- How it's counted: This is shown on the student dashboard. It takes the candidate's average AI Score (out of 10), multiplies it by 10, and caps it at 100. For example, an average AI score of 8.2 translates to an 82% Readiness Score. 

6. Hiring Recommendations (Recruiter Board)
- How it's counted: The system automatically categorizes candidates into three buckets based on a strict formula combining trust and skill:
    - Reject: Trust Score is strictly below 60.
    - Hire: Trust Score is 80 or higher AND AI Score is 7.0 or higher.
    - Consider: Everyone else who falls in between.

7. Global Dashboard Averages (Avg AI Score & Avg Trust Score)
- How they're counted: 
    - Platform Avg Trust Score: The mathematical average of the final Trust Scores of every completed interview on the platform.
    - Platform Avg AI Score: The average of every single submitted answer rating in the entire database (ignoring 0s, as per our recent fix).

8. Total Candidates
- How it's counted: The system counts the number of registered users who specifically have the student role and have successfully finished their onboarding (profileCompleted: true).
